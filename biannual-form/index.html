About this workshop +

Twice a year, we step out of the day-to-day and work on the business.

This two-part WMHQ intensive is designed to help you review what is actually working, identify the gaps slowing your growth, and build a clear plan for the next six months.

In Part 1: Review + Diagnose, we'll look at your numbers, offers, messaging, visibility, sales and current bottlenecks.

In Part 2: Decide + Build, you'll turn those insights into a focused six-month plan across your priorities, campaigns, content, cash and execution.

This is best experienced LIVE. You'll be able to ask questions as you make decisions, get immediate feedback when you're stuck between options, pressure-test your plan before you commit to it, and leave the session knowing exactly what to focus on next.

Replays will be available, but if you can make the live sessions, prioritise being in the room.
