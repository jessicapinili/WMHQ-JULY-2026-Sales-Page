Frequently Asked Questions

I'm just starting my business and haven't made any sales yet. Is WMHQ still right for me?
Yes. Around half of the women inside WMHQ are newer to business, while the other half have been building for years.

You do not need to already be making consistent sales to join. However, I do recommend having another source of income while your business is still being validated.

When your business needs to immediately pay every bill, it can create pressure that leads to rushed offers, inconsistent decisions and trying to force every post to convert.

Having income outside the business gives you the space to build your foundations properly, strengthen your messaging, validate your offer and learn how to generate sales without operating from panic.

WMHQ will help you build the strategy, skills and internal capacity required to turn your business into a reliable source of income.
Who is this actually for?
WMHQ is for female founders who have a business and want personalised support to scale it.

You may be newer in business or years in, but you are not here to keep guessing alone. You want clearer strategy, stronger messaging, better offers, more consistent sales, and support with the subconscious patterns that can show up when your business starts holding more.
Do I need to already be making a certain amount of money?
WMHQ is best suited for founders who already have something real to work with, an offer, audience, clients, customers, sales, or proof that people want what they do.

You do not need to be at a specific revenue level to join, but you do need to be ready to look at your business honestly, receive feedback, and implement.
Is there a minimum commitment?
Yes. WMHQ is a 6-month commitment at $450/month AUD.

After your first 6 months, your membership continues month-to-month unless you choose to cancel.
Why is it a 6-month commitment?
Because scaling a business is not a one-call fix.

We need enough time to look at your business properly, identify what is actually holding growth back, refine the strategy, strengthen the way you sell, and support the internal shifts required to hold more visibility, money, and responsibility.
What happens after the first 6 months?
After your initial 6-month commitment, your membership continues month-to-month.

You can stay inside WMHQ for ongoing support, refinement, coaching, feedback, and community as your business grows into its next stage.

Please make sure you read the details, understand the commitment, and DM Jess with any questions before joining. Read the full terms and conditions here.
How much time do I need each week?
Plan for around 2 to 4 hours per week.

That may include joining a live call, watching a replay, submitting something for feedback, reviewing your portal, or implementing the strategy given to you.

This is not about adding more to your plate. It is about knowing what actually matters next.
What can I submit for feedback?
You can submit things like your content, messaging, offers, sales pages, launch plans, buyer journey, visibility blocks, and subconscious patterns showing up in your business.

The goal is to help you see what is working, what is unclear, and what needs to shift.
Are the calls live?
Yes. WMHQ includes live calls.

You will receive weekly live calls for business and subconscious work. Replays are available if you cannot attend live.
What if I cannot attend the live calls?
You can watch the replay and still use the membership.

You can also submit for feedback and use the portal, vault, and resources to keep moving even when you cannot attend live.
Is this a course?
No. WMHQ is not just another course to consume.

It includes trainings and resources, but the main value is the personalised coaching, feedback, strategy, subconscious support, and the ability to have your actual business looked at properly.
