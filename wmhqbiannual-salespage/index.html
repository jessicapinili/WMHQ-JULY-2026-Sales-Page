4 reasons my online business income goes up every six months.

It is not luck, and it is not working harder. Twice a year I close the 99+ tabs and take a proper look at the business. These are the four things I check, and the exact four we work through together in the workshop.

Influence

I know exactly who I am talking to.

And why they would choose me. Clear messaging means fewer “I’ll think about it” replies and more yeses.

  • I know how to create clear messaging
  • I know exactly who I am speaking to
  • I know why they would choose me over anyone else
Visibility

My content puts me in front of buyers.

Not just likes. I check which posts and campaigns actually brought people in, then do more of those.

  • I know how to get my content in front of the right people
  • I know which campaigns bring in buyers, not just views
  • I know what to double down on and what to drop
Cash

My offers and sales process connect.

If people cannot see how to buy, they do not buy. I fix the gap between “love this” and paid.

  • I know how to build offers people want to buy
  • I know how my sales process connects, start to finish
  • I know where buyers drop off and how to fix it
Subconscious Mastery

I catch where fear is making my decisions.

The price I did not raise. The launch I pushed to next week. Naming it is what finally moves it.

  • I know when fear or self-doubt is shaping my decisions
  • I know when feeling stretched is stopping my follow-through
  • I know how to move what I keep putting off
